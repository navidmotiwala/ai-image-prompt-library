## Complete workflow

1. **Intake:** Gather front, back, side and detail reference photos, exact packaging artwork, approved claims, dimensions, brand palette and rights.
2. **Brief:** Define audience, funnel stage, message, placement and success metric.
3. **Concept:** Develop functional, emotional and editorial directions with a single visual promise each.
4. **Reference mapping:** Identify product truth, style reference and environment reference separately.
5. **Generate:** Start with one clean image. Keep changes small and controlled.
6. **Quality check:** Inspect geometry, label, scale, hands, physics, reflections, copy and claims.
7. **Variants:** Change one factor per test to understand differences.
8. **Recompose:** Adapt for 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9 without cutting key features.
9. **Export:** Save originals, layered edits, prompts, approval records and correct format.
10. **Measure:** Use actual performance data rather than guessing which image converts best.

## Repair commands

- `/fix-label`: Preserve original packaging artwork and correct all visible characters.
- `/fix-geometry`: Match product silhouette and dimensions to the source.
- `/fix-light`: Make light direction, contact shadows and reflections consistent.
- `/fix-hands`: Correct anatomy and believable contact with the product.
- `/fix-crop`: Recompose with full product and headline-safe area.
- `/fix-material`: Match actual glass, metal, fabric, leather or plastic properties.
- `/fix-consistency`: Lock the product, palette, camera height and light direction across variants.
- `/fix-claims`: Remove unsupported text, results and endorsements.
