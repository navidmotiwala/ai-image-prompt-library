# Product and ecommerce image prompts

[Back to README](../README.md)

These are custom slash labels for organizing prompts, not platform-native commands. Replace placeholders and supply references when accuracy matters.

## 1. Catalog hero

**Shortcut:** `/product /catalog /white /accuracy`

**Prompt**

> Photograph [PRODUCT] against pure white with accurate dimensions, color and packaging, soft contact shadow, front three-quarter perspective and no extra props.

**Best for:** product and ecommerce projects where this specific visual approach matches the brief.

**Customize:** Replace bracketed terms; specify target audience, brand palette, aspect ratio and the required output use. For reference-dependent work, attach an approved source image.

**Check:** Inspect text, product proportions, visual consistency, shadow direction and any implied factual claims.
