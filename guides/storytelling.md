**Shortcut:** `/story /six-panels /continuity`

**Prompt**

> Illustrate [STORY] in six chronological cinematic panels, preserving characters, wardrobe, setting and screen direction.

**Best for:** story and entertainment projects where this specific visual approach matches the brief.
