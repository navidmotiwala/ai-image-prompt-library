# Image quality control and troubleshooting

[Back to README](../README.md)

## Common failure: incorrect labels or logos

**Cause:** Image models may approximate text or invent markings.

**Fix:** Supply the original packaging reference, ask to preserve exact branding and inspect at full size. For critical text, composite verified artwork in a design editor.

## Common failure: product shape changes between images

**Cause:** Each generation can reinterpret geometry.

**Fix:** Use a fixed approved reference, preserve product silhouette and details, and change only the environment or lighting.

## Common failure: distorted hands or anatomy

**Fix:** Simplify the pose, use clear references, reduce occlusion and review fingers, joints and proportions carefully.

## Common failure: fake scientific diagrams

**Fix:** Start with verified source drawings or data. Use AI for presentation only. Have a qualified reviewer validate anatomy, engineering, chemistry and numbers.

## Common failure: unrealistic shadows and reflections

**Fix:** Specify one coherent light direction, contact shadows, reflective material properties and realistic perspective.

## Common failure: busy, unfocused composition

**Fix:** Reduce props, identify one focal point, define negative space and use one primary visual concept.

## Common failure: illegible generated typography

**Fix:** Generate background artwork with reserved copy space. Add final copy manually using the correct brand font.

## Common failure: poor cross-platform crops

**Fix:** Compose around safe zones, extend backgrounds where needed and review each final aspect ratio separately.

## Common failure: misleading before-and-after imagery

**Fix:** Use only genuine supplied comparison photographs. Do not fabricate outcomes or imply evidence that does not exist.

## Common failure: generic stock-photo appearance

**Fix:** Specify a concrete situation, place, action, relevant props and authentic behavior. Remove exaggerated expressions and artificial perfection.

## Pre-publication checklist

- [ ] Product or subject matches approved references
- [ ] Text and logos are accurate
- [ ] No unsupported product, health or performance claims
- [ ] Anatomy and geometry look plausible
- [ ] Lighting, shadows and reflections agree
- [ ] Image is properly cropped for its destination
- [ ] Important details remain legible at mobile size
- [ ] Rights, privacy and platform policies are considered
- [ ] Output is reviewed by a human before publication
